FIRST-TIMER TUTORIAL

HOW TO SKI
& SNOWBOARD.

A step-by-step beginner tutorial for your first day on snow, explained in plain language.

01 · BEFORE YOU DROP

Start on the flat

Keep your knees soft, look where you want to go, and make small movements. A lesson and helmet are always worth it.

02 · SKIERS

Pizza to stop

Point ski tips toward each other to slow down. Keep your hands forward and turn both skis together.

03 · SNOWBOARDERS

Find your edge

Use your heel edge to slow down, then gently roll onto your toe edge to turn across the slope. Stay loose.

04 · LIFTS & ETIQUETTE

Look uphill

Merge only when it is clear, stop at the side of the trail, and never sit in a blind landing or beneath a feature.

05 · FALLING

Fall safely

Relax, protect your head, and move to the edge when you can. On a snowboard, keep your board across the hill.

06 · YOUR FIRST RUN

Green means go

Choose an easy groomed run, take breaks, and celebrate control—not speed. Progress comes one turn at a time.
